The review protocol

Confidence is not evidence.

A tidy sentence can hide a changed denominator, an invented quote or a conclusion the source never made. Read closely. A suspicious answer can also turn out to be sound.

All records are fictional and complete for each case. This is scripted practice, with explanations after every verdict.
